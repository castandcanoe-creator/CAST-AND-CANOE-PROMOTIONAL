# Cast & Canoe — First-time Player Assessment (PRIVATE PREP)

Session: 2026-11-08 evening (CST), guest play as "Bullfrog" at castandcanoe.com, game v0.42.20, Casual mode ON (set by the maker). No account created. Result of the session: 5 fish landed in a row ("On Fire" badge), cooler 4/6, 1 fish in the aquarium; Kettle Pond fully explored and Reynolds Ponds visited via Journal → Lakes → Go to.

## Verdict

Strengths, ranked:
1. **Atmosphere and art** — dawn light (pink-gold, long shadows, teal water), Pine Island's yellow birches, the dark Deep Hole, dogs, the train and caboose. Dawn is the strongest look.
2. **Field-paper GUI and contextual prompts** — cream paper panels, hand-lettered signs, prompts like "Hold Space, let go in the gold", "Pet Beckham (E)", "Hop in the kayak (Tab)". The journal's fish rumours are delightful.
3. **Casual-mode fishing loop** — charge, tap in the gold, bite in ~10 s, catch card with weight/length/personality ("Scrappy", "Calm"), hold-up pose, plops, frequent badge toasts (First Cast, Fish On!, Early Bird, Who's a Good Dog?, On Fire…).
4. **Onboarding** — Andre's list (1 of 6: catch 3 fish at the Learning Dock) plus the How to Play journal teaches the game cold, in context, without pressure.
5. **Guest access** — journal, map, Pack, cooler, aquarium, shop and travel to other lakes all work with no account.

Top friction points, ranked:
1. **Movement is not taught in-game.** Pointer-ahead + held left click is essential (walking and boating) but no prompt explains it; arrow-key taps alone are slow and make the first walk to the dock a chore. Learned only from the maker.
2. **Non-Casual fishing timing** is unforgiving/confusing at first (nibbles vs "!", "too late / too soon" messages, reeling gauge with the red zone) — hard through any latency.
3. **Boat boarding is inconsistent** — kayak at Kettle Pond is Tab (E = "sit on the edge"), canoe at Reynolds was E, and a racked canoe at Reynolds did not launch.
4. **Wandering NPCs are hard to talk to** (Brooke repeatedly walked away before dialogue opened; same tendency noted with Otis), and some scenery that looks interactive is not (departure board, caboose door).
5. **Minor glitches** — aquarium took ~3 s to render the fish; lake menu occasionally said "as Andre" while the angler was Bullfrog, with fish counts differing from the Journal; at Reynolds the angler's name label floated over water with no visible canoe (racked-canoe state).

## Detail notes

- **Aesthetics:** warm greens/browns, teal water, yellow/gold accents (fish, plops, cast meter). Small lively animation: fish shadows, ducks, running dogs, canoe wake.
- **GUI:** Pack/Journal/Map/Options clear, shortcuts printed on buttons (P, J, M, C, H). One surprise: pressing Space while a Pack button had focus opened "Change your look".
- **Shop:** opens from a small bottom-centre prompt, not by clicking the caboose or pressing E at it — took several tries to find. Sell flow, tackle (Spinner, Jig 20, Spoon 35, Topwater frog 50, Minnow rig 60, Deep spoon 80), gear and lake community funds (fire pit, fireworks…) all read clearly.
- **Locals:** Otis's dialogue is charming and useful ("Old Kettle feeds after dark, down in the Deep Hole. A deep spoon, and give it time to sink."). Locals have train timetables (Andre to Cypress Bayou 9:52 PM, Brooke to West Hawk 11:16 PM).
- **Performance:** no crashes, loads in a few seconds, smooth in Chromium.
- **Not reachable as guest (by design):** Camps and Friends require sign-in; lake record book requires sign-in. Brooke dialogue not captured (she kept moving); the departure board is scenery, not a panel.

## Recommended audience

Cozy-game and fishing-game players; Stardew/Animal Crossing fans; families and first-time players (Casual mode on); anyone wanting a low-pressure shared lake. Less suited to fast-action players or anyone starting without Casual mode.

## Promo implications

- Lead with dawn/Pine Island/kayak imagery and the catch-card moment — they're the strongest assets.
- "Guest can do almost everything, free, no download" is a true, strong claim.
- Onboarding content (short-form/posts) should teach the pointer-hold movement early — it's the one thing the game itself doesn't teach, and it transforms the first session.

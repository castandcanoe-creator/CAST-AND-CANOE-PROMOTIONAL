## Goals

1. **Be ready to launch, not scrambling to launch.** Every asset, caption, and page a curious player, streamer, or journalist could ask for exists *before* the first public push.
2. **One message everywhere:** a cozy pixel fishing game, free, in your browser, better with friends. Every piece of material ends in "click and you're fishing" — no download, no install, no cost is the pitch.
3. **Build a reusable asset library**, not one-off posts. Screenshots, clips, and copy get captured once and sliced for the press kit, socials, and YouTube.

## Audiences

| Audience | Where they are | What hooks them |
|---|---|---|
| Cozy gamers | TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Steam-adjacent cozy communities | Handmade pixel art, journal/badges/aquarium collecting, campfire jam, zero pressure, "kind to every age" |
| Fishing-game fans | YouTube, Facebook groups, Reddit-adjacent | Real fish from real lakes, one-of-a-kind catches, catch cards with award ribbons, lake-specific species |
| Families / casual friend groups | Facebook, YouTube, word of mouth | Free, runs in a browser on phones and computers, private camps with an invite code, safe moderated chat, no skill barrier |

Secondary: small streamers and cozy-game press looking for something charming and easy to show an audience.

## Key hooks (ranked by expected pull)

1. **Free in the browser** — lowest friction of any pitch. Always in the first sentence or first 3 seconds.
2. **A pet at every lake** — Beckham & Olive, Shale, Port and Starboard, Juniper, Boudin. Named animals are inherently shareable.
3. **Book a private lake for your friends** — camps with a code (e.g. PINE-4K7Q). "Your own lake, just send the code."
4. **The maker is in the game** — Andre is played only by the game's maker; meeting him on a lake is meeting the developer.
5. **The fish rise at the top of the hour** — a built-in appointment mechanic at Kettle Pond; good for recurring "fishing o'clock" posts.
6. **Handmade** — hand pixel art, synthesised sounds, real fish from real lakes. Solo/handmade maker story.
7. **Collecting depth** — journal, badges, personal aquarium and cabin, catch cards with award ribbons.

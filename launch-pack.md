## FAQ

**Is it really free?**
Yes — free to play in the browser. No download, no install.

**What do I need to play?**
A web browser on a computer or a phone, and an internet connection. That's it.

**How do I play with my friends?**
See the one-pager below: fish the same shared lake, add each other as friends, or book a camp and share its code.

**Do I need an account?**
You can look around as a guest. Signing in (free) unlocks chat, friends, camps, and saves that follow you between devices.

**Is it suitable for kids / families?**
It's made to be kind to every age. Camp chat keeps links, phone numbers, and handles out; there's reporting and blocking, and private camps let a family or friend group keep a lake to themselves.

**Who makes it?**
One maker, by hand — pixel art, synthesised sounds, and frequent updates shaped by players. He appears in-game as Andre, one of the two guides.

**I found a bug / I have an idea.**
castandcanoe@gmail.com — updates are shaped by players, and the maker reads the reports.

**Will there be more content?**
Yes — new lakes, fish, and things to discover in regular updates; version notes are posted on castandcanoe.com.

## How to play with friends — one-pager outline

*Target: one press-kit page + one pinned social post. Keep to these beats with a screenshot each.*

1. **Just show up** — open castandcanoe.com. Everyone starts at Kettle Pond; if your friends are on the pond, you're already together.
2. **Add each other** — sign in, open Friends from the title page's FRIENDS sign or the Pack, add people you meet on a lake or share your friend link. Friends list shows where each friend is fishing, with Join to go to them.
3. **Or book a camp (best for a planned session)** — from the title page's CAMPS sign, book a camp at any lake; you get a code like PINE-4K7Q and a link. Send the code/link to your friends; they type it in and they're on your private lake.
4. **Set the gate** — camp gate can be open, ask, or shut; friends walk straight in when the gate is on Ask. Hosts can mute/remove.
5. **Talk while you fish** — signed-in players type in camp chat; names show in shirt colour, words appear over your angler's head; wave and dance emotes work everywhere, including for guests on public lakes.
6. **Good first session plan** — do Andre's list together at Kettle Pond, cheer each other's catches, feed Shale a small fish if you hop to Reynolds, be at the pond at the top of the hour when the fish rise.

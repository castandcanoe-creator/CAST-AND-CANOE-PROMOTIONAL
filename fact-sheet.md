**Game:** Cast & Canoe™

**One-liner:** A cozy pixel fishing game you play with friends, right in your browser.

**Description:**
Cast & Canoe is a cozy multiplayer pixel fishing game that runs entirely in the browser — free to play, no download, on computers and phones. Paddle out, cast a line, and see what's biting. Five lakes, from a little hub pond to a cypress bayou, each with its own fish, wildlife, locals, and secrets — and every catch is one of a kind. Fish together on a shared lake, or book a private camp and send your friends the code. Fill your journal, earn badges, keep your best catches in your own aquarium, and settle into your cabin. The art is pixel art made by hand, the sounds are synthesised, and the fish are real fish from real lakes.

**Genre:** Cozy / fishing / social multiplayer

**Platform:** Web browser (computers and phones). No download or install.

**Price:** Free to play.

**Players:** Solo or multiplayer. Shared public lakes plus bookable private camps for friend groups.

## Features

- Five lakes to explore, starting at Kettle Pond, the hub where every visit begins
- Fish with friends on a shared lake in real time, or on your own
- Bookable private camps: reserve a lake of your own and invite friends with a short code and link
- Friends system — see where your friends are fishing and join them
- In-lake chat with name-coloured text, speech over your angler's head, plus wave and dance emotes
- Two in-game guides, Andre and Brooke, who ride the Lakes Line between lakes — Andre is played only by the game's maker
- Every lake has its own locals to meet and a pet to befriend: dogs, a dock cat, a pair of ducks, a burro, and a pelican
- A journal, badges, and catch cards that pin each fish up with ribbons for its awards
- Your own aquarium and a cabin to fill
- Andre's list: a guided first hour in six chapters, so new players always have a next thing to try
- Campfire jam sessions with playable instruments (guitar, ukulele, harmonica)
- Handmade pixel art and synthesised sounds; real fish from real lakes
- Actively updated — shaped by its players (v0.42.20 at time of writing)

## The lakes

| Lake | Notes for press/social use |
|---|---|
| Kettle Pond | The hub. Everyone starts here. Station, caboose shop, Learning Dock, Pine Island, the Deep Hole (home of Old Kettle, a huge pike). Pets: Beckham and Olive (dogs). Locals include Otis at the station. Fish rise for ten minutes at the top of every hour. |
| Reynolds Ponds | Beaver country — Mags counts the beavers. Pet: Shale, Hollis's grey cat, on the dock (loves a small fish). |
| West Hawk | Dale has trolled here for thirty summers. Pets: Port and Starboard, Dale's ducks, who follow you along the beach in a line. |
| Slickrock | Wade's lake. Pet: Juniper, a burro who loves an apple and brays at thunder. |
| The bayou | Cypress bayou; Gus's deck and Celestine's pirogue. Pet: Boudin, the pelican (toss him a small fish). The bait shop is the shop here. |

## Developer note

Cast & Canoe is made by hand by its maker, who also appears in the game as Andre, one of the two guides — if you meet Andre on an open lake, that's really him. Brooke, the other guide, is the Captain on the open lakes. Updates are frequent and written as short stories in the version notes, and new lakes, fish, and things to discover are shaped by the players.
